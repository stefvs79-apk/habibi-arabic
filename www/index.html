Cultural & Linguistic Note

In Arabic hospitality, greetings like أَهْلًا وَسَهْلًا (Ahlan wa sahlan) literally mean "You have arrived among family and trodden on smooth ground." The natural reply substitutes the speaker's pronoun: أَهْلًا بِكَ (Ahlan bika / Fusha) or أَهْلِين فِيك (Ahleyn feek / Levantine).
